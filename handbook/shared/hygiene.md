# Hygiene

The non-rebreathing T-valve stops exhaled air from staying in the circuit. Under normal
conditions it is enough to:

- give each participant a **new single-use filtered mouthpiece**;
- clean or replace the **coupler between the mouthpiece and the T-valve**;
- disinfect everything the participant touches: mouse, keyboard, microphone stand, desk.

## Extended protocol (used during COVID-19, 2021)

The full protocol from the 2022 guide, for when stricter hygiene is required.

**Before the first session of the day**

- Get supplies: dish towels, paper towels, water and disposable cups, mouthpieces,
  ribbed tubes, alcohol wipes, 70 % alcohol, gloves, hand disinfectant.
- Use each ribbed tube once per day. Rotate 4–5 of them and let them air out between
  days; use a hair dryer to ventilate if needed.
- Air the room. Wipe down every touched surface: tables, door and window handles,
  keyboard, mouse, microphone stand, the device and cables, and the drying surface.
- Put out an unopened mouthpiece.

**After each participant**

1. Put on gloves and open the windows (about 15 minutes).
2. Take the breathing circuit out of the device and take it apart.
3. Rinse both tubes with 70 % alcohol for about 30 s, wetting all couplers.
4. Take apart the T-valve and rinse each part. **Be gentle with the silicone
   diaphragms.**
5. Dry the parts on a clean towel. Wipe down the mouse, keyboard, stand, desk, chair and
   windowsill.
6. Reassemble when dry, with both spiral diaphragms facing the same way (see
   [Breathing circuit](breathing-circuit.md#t-valve-hans-rudolph-1410)). Fit a fresh,
   dry ribbed tube.

**After the last participant**

- Unplug the device, clean everything as above, leave the parts to dry and air the room.
- Restock supplies for the next day.
